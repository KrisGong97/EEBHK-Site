Company Profile

China Railway Electrification Engineering Group (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. is the Hong Kong subsidiary of China Railway Electrification Engineering Group under China Railway Group Limited, serving as the Group's key window and delivery platform for the Hong Kong, Macao and overseas markets.

Founded in 1958 alongside the construction of China's first electrified railway — the Baoji–Chengdu line — the Group has grown into a large enterprise integrating engineering construction, survey and design, R&D, industrial manufacturing, testing and inspection, engineering supervision, materials trading and logistics, and O&M services. It stands as the national leader in China's electrified railway and urban rail transit construction, and a key member of China Railway Group Limited, a Fortune Global 500 company in both revenue and brand.

The Group has participated in over 70% of China's electrified railways, over 60% of its high-speed railways and over 70% of its urban rail transit projects. It operates the world's largest production base for railway electrification and metro catenary components, with business spanning China and more than 20 overseas countries and regions.

Building on the Group's six decades of expertise, the Hong Kong company specialises in the design, supply, installation and commissioning of rail power supply systems, E&M works and platform screen door systems. It plays a deep role in multiple key MTR infrastructure projects — including the largest power supply renewal in MTR history — bringing world-class technology and management to Hong Kong's urban mobility.

Milestones

1958

The Group's predecessor was born with the electrification of the Baoji–Chengdu railway, China's first electrified railway.

1974

The Railway Electrification Engineering Bureau was formally established as China's dedicated force for railway electrification.

1980s–1990s

Delivered the Datong–Qinhuangdao line, China's first unit heavy-haul coal railway, and the Guangzhou–Shenzhen line, China's first 160 km/h quasi-high-speed railway.

2003–2012

Completed the Qinhuangdao–Shenyang passenger dedicated line (China's first), the Beijing–Tianjin Intercity (China's first 350 km/h HSR), the Beijing–Shanghai HSR (a world benchmark), and the Harbin–Dalian HSR (the world's first high-speed railway in severe cold regions).

2020

As leader of a joint venture, awarded the MTR Tsuen Wan, Kwun Tong and Island lines power supply renewal contract worth HKD 3.032 billion — the largest power supply overhaul in MTR history.

Recent Years

Awarded MTR Contract 1254 for platform screen doors on the Tung Chung Line Extension, Tuen Mun South Extension and East Rail Line — the Group's first NEC-contract project, further strengthening its Hong Kong presence.

Qualifications & Honours

The Group holds premium-grade general contracting qualifications for railway and building construction, multiple general and specialist contracting qualifications covering E&M, communications, municipal and power engineering, as well as Grade-A design qualifications.
