About Us

About Us

China Railway Electrification Engineering Group (Hong Kong) Co., Ltd. is the Hong Kong subsidiary of China Railway Electrification Engineering Group, a member of China Railway Group Limited. Founded in 1958 alongside the construction of China's first electrified railway — the Baoji–Chengdu line — the Group has grown into a large enterprise integrating engineering construction, survey and design, R&D, industrial manufacturing, and O&M services, standing as the national leader in China's electrified railway and urban rail transit construction.

Building on the Group's six decades of expertise, the Hong Kong company specialises in the design, supply, installation and commissioning of rail power supply systems, E&M works and platform screen door systems, playing a deep role in multiple key MTR infrastructure projects.
