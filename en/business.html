Business Segments

業務板塊

Four-Electric System Integration

The Group has built technical systems for heavy-haul, long-trunk and high-speed railways, fully mastering high-speed electrification design and construction, system integration management, and a proprietary Chinese high-speed railway traction power supply technology platform.

  • Overhead catenary systems: design, construction and integration — products used in over 70% of China's operating rail transit lines
  • Traction substation systems: substation design, equipment installation and commissioning
  • Power systems: power supply and distribution for railways and urban rail transit
  • Communication & signalling systems for rail transit

Urban Rail E&M Works

Deeply rooted in the MTR market, the company delivers highly complex works on live lines operating over 19 hours a day — covering the design, supply, installation and testing of power supply renewals, as well as platform screen door systems — with proven interface coordination capabilities across E&M and civil contractors.

R&D & Manufacturing

The Group operates the world's largest production base for railway electrification and metro catenary equipment, and actively applies big data, the Internet, 5G and BeiDou navigation technologies to advance intelligent construction and smart site management, setting new benchmarks for four-electric HSR engineering.

O&M Services

Full life-cycle operation, maintenance and management of rail transit facilities, supported by self-developed equipment such as catenary dynamic inspection vehicles and inspection robots, ensuring long-term safe, reliable and efficient operations.
