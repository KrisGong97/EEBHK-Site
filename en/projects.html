MTR Three-Line Power Supply Renewal (2020–2028)

A joint venture led by the Group was awarded the power supply system renewal of the MTR Tsuen Wan, Kwun Tong and Island lines, valued at HKD 3.032 billion. These three lines — Hong Kong's first metro lines, in service for nearly 40 years — carry about half of the MTR's 5 million daily passenger trips. Spanning 49 km and 39 stations, the works cover the design, supply, installation and testing of high- and low-voltage power systems. With lines operating over 19 hours a day, the difficulty, duration and complexity of the renewal are unprecedented in MTR history.

MTR Platform Screen Doors, Contract 1254

Through a joint venture, the Group was awarded the design, supply, installation and commissioning of platform screen doors for the Tung Chung Line Extension, Tuen Mun South Extension and East Rail Line — covering seven stations: Tung Chung West, Tung Chung East, Siu Ho Wan, Tuen Mun South, Tuen Mun Area 16, Hung Shui Kiu and Kwu Tung — including interface design and programme coordination with other E&M and civil contractors. This is the Group's first MTR platform screen door contract and its first project under the NEC contract model.
